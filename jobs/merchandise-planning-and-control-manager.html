Merchandise Planning and Control Manager

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company (department store and supermarket) is looking for a Merchandise Planning and Control Manager to manage its Merchandise Planning and Control team and help the company reach its sales and profit goals.

What you'll do

  • Lead the preparation of sales and purchase budgets per store, department and classification
  • Make sure purchase budgets match sales budgets and the company's profit and gross margin targets
  • Work with merchandising, supermarket purchasing and operations, IT, accounting and top management on budgets, reports and inventory matters
  • Track actual purchases, markdowns, monthly stocks and gross margin against plan, and give timely reports to management
  • Analyze inventory adjustments and oversee the recording of supermarket inventory movements
  • Oversee physical inventory taking and the timely submission of monthly reports
  • Manage the Merchandise Planning and Control team (Merchandise Planning and Control officer, stock control supervisor, merchandising analysts and stock control clerks): assign and schedule work, train, coach and evaluate staff, and manage the department budget

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Management, Economics, Statistics or a related field
  • At least 5–7 years in merchandise planning, inventory control or sales analysis in retail, including 2–3 years as a supervisor or manager
  • Strong knowledge of retail budgeting and metrics (open-to-buy, stock-to-sales, markdowns, gross margin)
  • Advanced Excel skills (pivot tables, lookups); experience with retail/ERP systems is a plus
  • Good at working with buyers, merchandising, IT and accounting/finance teams
  • Strong leadership and report-writing skills
  • Detail-oriented, with high integrity in handling confidential data

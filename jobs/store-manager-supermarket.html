Store Manager (Supermarket)

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company is looking for a Supermarket Store Manager to lead its store team and drive sales, efficiency and customer service.

What you'll do

  • Drive store sales to meet or beat daily, weekly and monthly targets
  • Manage the store budget and control costs such as payroll, utilities and supplies
  • Oversee stock levels, ordering and receiving to avoid out-of-stocks and reduce losses from theft, spoilage or errors
  • Keep the store clean, organized and well-merchandised, with promo displays set up on time and correct prices
  • Hire, train, schedule, coach and evaluate store staff and supervisors
  • Handle escalated customer concerns and keep checkout lines moving during peak hours
  • Coordinate with buyers, suppliers, concessionaires, security and maintenance for smooth daily operations
  • Canvass and monitor competitors' prices, promos and product offerings regularly, and report findings so pricing stays competitive

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Retail Management, Marketing or a related field
  • At least 3–5 years of managerial or supervisory experience in retail, preferably in a supermarket or grocery
  • A proven record of meeting sales goals, managing budgets and reducing shrinkage
  • Strong leadership, coaching and conflict-resolution skills
  • Working knowledge of POS and inventory systems, and MS Office (Excel, Word, Outlook)
  • Knowledge of food safety standards (HACCP) is an advantage
  • Willing to work weekends, evenings and holidays, and to be assigned to any of our branches within Metro Manila

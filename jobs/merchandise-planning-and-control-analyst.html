Merchandise Planning and Control Analyst

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company (department store and supermarket) is looking for a Merchandise Planning and Control Analyst to prepare, monitor and analyze sales, purchase budgets and inventory levels.

What you'll do

  • Prepare sales and purchase budgets per department and classification, using growth, industry and inflation data
  • Compute average stock, monthly stock and planned markdowns
  • Monitor sales, purchases, markdowns and open orders per store against the merchandise plan
  • Analyze monthly sales and inventory reports and recommend inventory adjustments
  • Compute economic order quantity (EOQ), re-order points and cost standards
  • Evaluate how well promotions perform, working with the marketing team
  • Maintain historical sales, purchase and markdown records

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Management, Economics, Statistics or a related field
  • 1–3 years in merchandise planning, inventory control, budgeting or sales analysis, preferably in retail (fresh graduates with strong analytical skills are welcome to apply)
  • Advanced Excel skills (pivot tables, lookups, formulas)
  • Good understanding of inventory concepts such as stock-to-sales, markdowns, EOQ and re-order points
  • Detail-oriented and accurate with numbers
  • High integrity in handling confidential data
  • Willing to be assigned to any of our branches or offices within Metro Manila

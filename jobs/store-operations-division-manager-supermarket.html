Store Operations Division Manager (Supermarket)

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company is looking for a Store Operations Division Manager to lead its supermarket operations, including the store, receiving/warehouse and checkout teams, with the goal of hitting sales targets, giving fast and friendly service and keeping costs and losses low.

What you'll do

  • Lead supermarket store managers and the receiving/warehouse and checkout (counter) teams across the division
  • Drive sales and fast, courteous service, handle escalated complaints, and monitor each store's sales performance
  • Oversee receiving, stocking and stock rotation (FIFO) of fresh, chilled and dry goods to reduce spoilage, expiry and shrinkage
  • Keep shelves full, displays neat and promotions on time, working with supermarket purchasing and merchandising
  • Manage checkout operations, including cashier scheduling, bagging supplies, POS machines and the safekeeping of cash
  • Prepare and control the division budget, and enforce food safety, sanitation and store standards
  • Hire, train, evaluate and develop managers and supervisors, and keep discipline and morale high

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Management, Retail Management or a related field; an MBA is a plus
  • At least 8–10 years of retail store operations experience, including 5+ years managing multiple supermarket or grocery stores
  • Hands-on experience with fresh/perishables handling, receiving and checkout operations
  • A proven record of meeting sales targets, controlling expenses and reducing shrinkage and spoilage
  • Knowledge of food safety and sanitation standards (e.g. HACCP) is an advantage
  • Strong leadership, decision-making and customer-service skills, and good knowledge of POS and inventory systems
  • Willing to work weekends and holidays, and to visit stores within Metro Manila regularly

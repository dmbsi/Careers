Merchandise Planning and Analysis Supervisor

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company (department store and supermarket) is looking for a Merchandise Planning and Analysis Supervisor to keep sales, purchases and inventory in balance.

What you'll do

  • Monitor sales, purchase budgets and inventory levels across merchandising departments
  • Check planned sales, purchases, markdowns, monthly and average stocks, and initial gross margins for accuracy
  • Prepare quarterly, semi-annual and yearly sales reviews for management
  • Prepare monthly concessionaire analysis reports
  • Oversee physical inventory taking
  • Keep historical sales, purchase and inventory data, and follow up system-generated reports
  • Lead, schedule, train and evaluate a team of analysts

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Accounting, Management, Statistics or a related field
  • At least 3 years in merchandise planning, inventory control or sales analysis in retail, with at least 1 year in a supervisory role
  • Strong skills with numbers and good knowledge of retail metrics (sell-through, stock-to-sales, gross margin)
  • Advanced Excel skills (pivot tables, lookups); experience with retail/ERP systems is a plus
  • Good at coordinating with buyers and merchandising teams
  • Detail-oriented, with high integrity in handling confidential data
  • Willing to be assigned to any of our branches or offices within Metro Manila

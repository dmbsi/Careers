Store Operations Division Manager (Department Store)

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company is looking for a Store Operations Division Manager to lead its department store operations, including the selling floors, warehouse/receiving and counter (cashiering) teams, with the goal of hitting sales targets, giving excellent customer service and keeping costs low.

What you'll do

  • Lead department store managers, the traffic (warehouse/receiving) manager and counter managers across the division
  • Drive sales and excellent customer service, handle escalated complaints, and monitor each store's sales performance
  • Improve store layout, displays, promotions, selling skills and product knowledge with the merchandising, store design and training teams
  • Oversee receiving, tagging, warehouse space and stock requisitions (FIFO) to reduce losses and keep inventory at the right level
  • Manage counter/cashiering operations, including staff scheduling, supplies, POS machines and the safekeeping of cash and gift cheques
  • Prepare and control the division budget, work with leasing on concessionaire matters, and improve policies and procedures
  • Hire, train, evaluate and develop managers and supervisors, and keep discipline and morale high

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Management, Retail Management or a related field; an MBA is a plus
  • At least 8–10 years of retail store operations experience, including 5+ years managing multiple stores or departments
  • Department store experience, including warehousing/receiving, cashiering and concessionaire management
  • A proven record of meeting sales targets, controlling expenses and reducing shrinkage
  • Good eye for visual merchandising, store layout and promotions
  • Strong leadership, decision-making and customer-service skills, and good knowledge of POS and inventory systems
  • Willing to work weekends and holidays, and to visit stores within Metro Manila regularly

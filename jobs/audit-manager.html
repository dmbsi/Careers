Audit Manager

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company (department store and supermarket) is looking for an Audit Manager to lead its internal audit team, plan and oversee financial and operational audits across the main office and branches, and report findings to management.

What you'll do

  • Design and update audit programs, and prepare the annual audit plan and interim audit schedules
  • Lead and supervise the Financial and Operations Audit Supervisors and Junior Auditors, and review their working papers
  • Review internal controls and recommend improvements to systems and procedures
  • Conduct branch audits covering cash/POS, inventory counts, shrinkage and concessionaire accounts
  • Test compliance with company policies and procedures
  • Prepare and present audit reports to management
  • Manage the department budget, staff schedules, performance reviews and training
  • Coordinate with other department heads and attend management meetings

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Accountancy, Finance or a related field; CPA preferred (CIA is a plus)
  • At least 5 years of internal or external audit experience, including 2+ years supervising auditors
  • Audit experience in retail, a department store or a supermarket is a strong advantage
  • Strong knowledge of internal controls and cash and inventory audit procedures
  • Good leadership, communication and report-writing skills
  • Willing to work on-site and travel to branches within Metro Manila as needed

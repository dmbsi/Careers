Junior Auditor (Financial)

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company (department store and supermarket) is looking for a detail-oriented Junior Auditor to help protect company assets and keep financial records accurate across its stores.

What you'll do

  • Conduct surprise cash counts of cashier funds, POS/cash-in-drawer, sales for deposit, and disbursement and collection funds
  • Check accounting and financial data through reconciliations, vouching and review of books and records
  • Test compliance with company financial policies, memos and procedures
  • Audit accounting, finance, cashiering, and credit and collection operations
  • Take part in inventory and fixed asset counts to confirm assets exist and are kept safe
  • Prepare audit findings and reports for the Audit Supervisor
  • Help draft audit programs and recommend process improvements
  • Coordinate with department managers, branch staff and fellow auditors

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Accountancy or a related accounting course; CPA or board passer is a plus
  • 0–2 years of experience in audit, accounting or cashiering; retail, department store or supermarket experience is an advantage
  • Fresh graduates with audit-related OJT are welcome to apply
  • Honest, organized and detail-oriented
  • Good MS Excel skills
  • Willing to travel to different branches within Metro Manila

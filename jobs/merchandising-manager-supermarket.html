Merchandising Manager (Supermarket)

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company is looking for a Merchandising Manager to lead its supermarket merchandising and keep it competitive.

What you'll do

  • Build and carry out the merchandising strategy across all supermarket categories (Fresh, Dry Grocery, Non-Food, Personal Care)
  • Manage category performance: sales, gross margin, inventory turnover and the product assortment
  • Set competitive prices and plan promotions and seasonal campaigns
  • Approve planograms and oversee shelf space, displays and in-store presentation
  • Work with buying, warehouse and store teams to keep shelves stocked, rotate stock and reduce waste
  • Negotiate trading terms, rebates and listing fees with suppliers
  • Lead and train buyers and store merchandising teams

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Economics, Supply Chain Management or a related field. An MBA or a retail or procurement certification is a plus.
  • At least 5–7 years in retail or FMCG, including 2–3 years as a supervisor or manager (for example Senior Buyer, Category Manager or Assistant Merchandising Manager)
  • A record of growing sales and margins in supermarket categories
  • Strong Excel skills (pivot tables, VLOOKUP) and experience with ERP or POS systems
  • Strong negotiation and communication skills, and high integrity
  • Willing to visit stores and check competitors regularly, and to work longer hours during inventory counts and peak seasons
  • Willing to be assigned to any of our branches or offices within Metro Manila

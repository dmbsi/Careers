Buyer (Supermarket)

Full-time | On-site | Metro Manila only

All positions are based in branches within Metro Manila and are open to Metro Manila residents only. We are posting these openings on behalf of our client.

An established Philippine retail company is looking for a Supermarket Buyer to handle purchasing for an assigned product category and keep its product mix competitive. The category will be assigned based on your experience.

What you'll do

  • Select and buy products for an assigned supermarket category (e.g. Fresh, Dry Grocery, Frozen/Chilled, Beverages, Personal Care or Household) based on trends, customer demand and market checks
  • Negotiate with suppliers on cost, payment terms, returns of bad orders, listing fees and rebates
  • Manage the purchasing budget and stock levels to avoid out-of-stocks, overstocking and expired goods
  • Set retail prices to meet margin targets while staying competitive
  • Plan supplier-funded promotions, flyer items and seasonal buying
  • Monitor supplier performance (fill rate, on-time delivery, quality)
  • Coordinate with store operations, warehouse, marketing and finance

What we're looking for

  • A bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Supply Chain Management or Economics
  • At least 2–3 years in retail purchasing, procurement or merchandising, preferably in a supermarket or FMCG (fast-moving consumer goods) company; experience in any grocery or non-food category is welcome
  • A proven record of successful supplier negotiations
  • Advanced Excel skills (VLOOKUP, pivot tables) for sales and margin analysis
  • Good knowledge of local consumer preferences and seasonal trends
  • High integrity in dealing with suppliers
  • Willing to be assigned to any of our branches or offices within Metro Manila

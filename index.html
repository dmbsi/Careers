An established Philippine retail company is looking for a Supermarket Store Manager to lead its store team and drive sales, efficiency and customer service.

  • A proven record of meeting sales goals, managing budgets and reducing shrinkage
  • Strong leadership, coaching and conflict-resolution skills
  • Working knowledge of POS and inventory systems, and MS Office (Excel, Word, Outlook)
  • Knowledge of food safety standards (HACCP) is an advantage
  • Willing to work weekends, evenings and holidays, and to be assigned to any of our branches within Metro Manila
